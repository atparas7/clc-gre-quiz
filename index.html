Total testing time is about 1 hour 58 minutes. The Verbal and Quant scores are reported separately; added together they give the total of 260–340 that many programs quote.

Step 1

Your raw score = the number of questions you got right

  • Every question is worth exactly 1 raw point, whether it is easy or hard.
  • No penalty for a wrong or blank answer — never leave a question empty; guess if you must.
  • Questions with several answers (“select all that apply”, two- or three-blank Text Completion, Sentence Equivalence) count as correct only if every part is right. There is no partial credit.
  • Numeric Entry: any equivalent value is correct (on the CLC tests, 35/2, 70/4 and 17.5 are all accepted).
  • Verbal and Quant each have 12 + 15 = 27 questions, so each raw score is from 0 to 27.
Step 2

The test is section-level adaptive

The first section of each measure is the same difficulty for everyone. How well you do on it decides how hard your second section will be.

Section 112 questions · medium difficulty
→
Hard Section 2strong Section 1 · scores up to 170
Medium Section 2average Section 1
Easy Section 2weak Section 1 · lower ceiling

A harder second section is worth more: ETS adjusts the conversion (“equating”) so that the same number of correct answers gives a higher score on the hard section than on the easy one. That is why Section 1 matters so much — it decides how high you can go.

Step 3

Raw score → 130–170 scaled score

ETS converts the raw score to the 130–170 scale using your second-section level. ETS does not publish its exact tables, so the CLC tests use the calibrated tables below — the score you see is an estimate, accurate to about ±2–3 points.

How CLC routes you: Full Mock Tests 2–5 — Section 1 score 0–5 → Easy, 6–9 → Medium, 10–12 → Hard. Full Mock Test 1 — 8–12 → Hard, otherwise Medium. Verbal Adaptive Tests — Module 1 score 8–12 → Harder, otherwise Easier (scored with the Medium column). Grey cells are scores that cannot occur on that route in Full Mock Tests 2–5.

Example: 20 correct out of 27 gives 152 after an Easy Section 2, 157 after a Medium one and 160 after a Hard one.

Step 4

Percentile rank — how you compare

Your score report also shows a percentile: the percentage of test takers who scored lower than you. Official ETS data for everyone who tested from July 2022 to June 2025 (mean scores: Verbal 151.4, Quant 157.6):

Notice the difference between the two measures: 160 is the 82nd percentile in Verbal but only the 50th in Quant, and even a perfect 170 in Quant is the 89th percentile, because so many test takers score high in Quant.

On this platform

Which CLC tests give you a GRE score

  • Full Mock Tests 1–5 (Quant) and Verbal Adaptive Tests 1–6: full GRE-style score report — 130–170 score, percentile, raw score per section.
  • Topic tests and Units: raw score and percent correct (they practise one topic, so a GRE score would not be meaningful).
  • Every result is saved on the Progress & Report page (best GRE Quant and best GRE Verbal) and sent to your teacher.
Good to know
  • GRE scores are valid for 5 years.
  • With ScoreSelect you choose which test dates to send to schools.
  • Analytical Writing is scored by a trained human rater and the e-rater® scoring engine.
